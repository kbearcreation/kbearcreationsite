The Plan

Currently I am working on a blog that will allow people close to us to subscribe and get updates on what we're up to. This will include the story of Diane's fight with metastatic breast cancer. It will also include the joy that we try to find every day. We don't know what the future holds, but we are going to take every opportunity we can to embrace the things we love, the people who bring happiness into our lives and the wonders that the world holds. Now, more than ever, we see how precious our time here is, and we look forward to sharing with you the story of what we do with it.

Please stay tuned to the Bare Arts website at https://barearts.store, which is the temporary placeholder site for what will be a gallery for local, independent artists, a store where you can purchase their work and also a home for my blogs. The Bare Arts blog will cover the artists on the site as well as others that I meet along the way, events, openings and cool stuff that I want to share far and wide. This site will also be home to the private blog that is for our friends and loved ones who want to follow what is happening with us on a day-to-day basis. Rather than feeding the social media machine with the content we create, we hope that this will be a better way to let those who care about us stay engaged, whether they are nearby or far away.

Thank you for coming this far, and I hope that you will stick around and join us as we begin to share our journey, whatever it may be. Thank you so much.
~ Kevin
